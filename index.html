the five phases

your emotional weather report

Every cycle has a story arc. Here's the cheat sheet — no med degree, no pretending you understood. Just vibes & moves.

days 1–5
period days
She might be crampy, tired, and just want the couch and a warm blanket.
Bring snacks. Make zero plans. Be a human heating pad.
days 6–12
follicular phase
Energy returns, mood lifts. She's curious, social, and ready for stuff again.
Best for date nights, big convos, new plans. Ride the wave.
days 13–15
ovulation window
Peak confidence. She's glowing, talkative, magnetic to be around.
Take her somewhere nice. Say the sweet things. Main character era.
days 16–20
early luteal
Mood settles. Still sweet, but quietly needs more reassurance than usual.
Check in more. Small gestures hit hard. Be consistent, not flashy.
days 21–28
pms zone handle w/ care
Emotions are real & valid. May feel sensitive, bloated, or easily overwhelmed.
Don't argue small stuff. Ask "what do you need?" — and mean it.

the bro code

six rules for every phase

Memorize these. Stick them to the inside of your skull. You'll thank me on day 23.

01
never invalidate her feelings
Even if it's "just hormones," it's still real to her. Listen without solving. She often needs to be heard, not fixed.
02
small gestures, big return
A warm drink, her favourite snack, or a "thinking of you" text on a hard day? That's boyfriend-of-the-year behaviour.
03
pick your battles wisely
In the PMS zone, everything feels bigger. Save the tricky talks for follicular week — energy & openness are both high there.
04
match her ovulation era
She's glowing & confident — meet that energy. Compliments, quality time, and a little adventure all land best in this window.
05
consistency > grand gestures
Showing up steady every day across the whole cycle beats one big romantic moment. Be reliable, not just charming.
06
ask, don't assume
"What do you need right now?" is the most powerful sentence in your arsenal. Use it. Mean it. Then actually do the thing.
